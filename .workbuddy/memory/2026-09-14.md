### Decisions worth remembering
- A Sending Plan stores a *snapshot* of the configuration; `adjust_plan` validates against the
  plan's snapshot, so loosening constraints requires re-proposing.
- Adjusting a confirmed action invalidates its Confirmation with reason `adjusted`; re-confirming
  unchanged work is idempotent, changed work renews with reason `renewed`/`expired`.
- `_require_immediate_kind` runs before the mailbox-enabled check so a scheduled Confirmation
  reports the unverified native-scheduling capability instead of a misleading "execution disabled".
- Elapsed confirmed times pause with `confirmation_expired` and an explicit "replacement time
  required, never an immediate send" detail (`_expired_detail`).
